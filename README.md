# CityU-Tracker
A community-driven, anonymous admissions tracker built for students at my university in Macau. The platform allows students to share their graduate school application results—such as offers, rejections, GPA, GRE/IELTS scores, and program details—while protecting their privacy. The goal is to give future applicants transparent, reliable insights into where students with similar backgrounds have been admitted, and to help them make informed decisions when applying abroad.

Unlike traditional agencies or limited official reports, this tracker is open, student-centered, and data-driven, built to grow naturally as more users contribute.


## 🌟Core Features
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------

### Anonymous application submissions
Students can upload their offers, rejections, scholarships, and test scores without revealing identity.

### Personal application tracker
Users can keep their own applications private or choose to share them publicly.

### Public admissions database
A searchable, filterable list of past applications—by country, program, year, and keywords.

### Flexible program entry
Students may type any program name (no restrictive predefined list), enabling coverage of global programs.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
### Insights & statistics
#### Aggregated data showing:
Popular countries/regions
Acceptance rates
Average GPA / GRE / IELTS ranges
Trends across faculties and graduation years

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
### Future extensions
Alumni interviews, guidance content, collaboration with agencies, premium analytics, and workshops.
